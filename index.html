Support

Droproom turns your phone into a temporary local file share. People nearby join from a browser — no app, no account — and files move directly between devices, never through a cloud.

Droproom 把你的手機變成一個臨時的區域網路檔案分享。身邊的人用瀏覽器加入—— 不用裝 App、不用帳號——檔案直接在裝置之間傳送，不經雲端。

Quick answers · 常見問題

  • Nobody can join? Everyone must be on the same Wi-Fi, and that network must allow devices to talk to each other. Public, hotel, school, and office networks often block this (client isolation). The most reliable fallback is a personal hotspot.
    沒人連得上？所有人要在同一個 Wi-Fi，且該網路要允許裝置互連。公共／飯店／學校／公司網路常會封鎖（client isolation）。最可靠的替代方案是開個人熱點。
  • From a computer: type the address shown under the QR code into the browser's address bar, then tap "Let in" on the phone when asked.
    電腦端：把 QR code 下方的位址輸入瀏覽器網址列，然後在手機上按「讓他進來」。
  • Sharing stops in the background: iOS pauses network listeners when an app leaves the foreground. Keep Droproom open while people transfer; it resumes automatically when you come back.
    切到背景會暫停：iOS 會暫停背景 App 的網路監聽。傳輸期間請讓 Droproom 保持開啟；回到 App 會自動恢復。
  • Is it encrypted? Transfers stay inside your local network but are not encrypted — share with people you trust.
    有加密嗎？傳送只在你的區域網路內，但未加密——請與你信任的人分享。
  • Free limit: the free tier includes 5 sharing days — a day only counts when a file is actually received, and unused days never expire. A one-time purchase removes the limit.
    免費額度：5 個分享日——只有實際收到檔案的那天才計入，沒用完的天數不會過期。一次性購買即可解除限制。
