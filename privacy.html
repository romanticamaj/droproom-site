Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-08 · Droproom for iOS

The short version

Droproom collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no Droproom-operated server — we could not see your files or your activity even if we wanted to.

Droproom 不收集任何資料。沒有帳號、沒有分析、沒有追蹤，也沒有 Droproom 營運的伺服器——我們想看也看不到你的檔案或使用行為。

What happens to your files

Files you share move directly between devices over your local network (Wi-Fi or personal hotspot). They are stored on the host phone only while a share is open, and are removed from the app when you end it. They never pass through any external server. Transfers on the local network are not encrypted; other devices on the same network may be able to observe them, so share with people you trust.

你分享的檔案只在你的區域網路（Wi-Fi 或個人熱點）內、直接在裝置之間傳送。 檔案只在分享進行期間存放於主機手機上，結束分享即從 App 中移除，全程不經任何外部伺服器。 區域網路內的傳送未加密，同一網路上的其他裝置可能觀察到流量，請與信任的人分享。

What we don't do

No personal data is collected, stored, or shared. No identifiers are read. No third-party SDKs for analytics or advertising are included. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple; we receive no payment details.

不收集、不儲存、不分享任何個人資料；不讀取任何識別碼；不內建任何分析或廣告 SDK。付款完全由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得任何付款資訊。

Permissions the app asks for

Local network (to serve files to people you invite), camera (to scan QR codes and take photos or videos you choose to share), photo library and microphone (only when you add media to a share), and location-when-in-use (Apple requires it to read the Wi-Fi name, which is shown only so you can tell guests which network to join — location is never used or stored).

本機網路（提供檔案給你邀請的人）、相機（掃描 QR code 與拍攝你選擇分享的 照片影片）、照片圖庫與麥克風（僅在你加入媒體時使用）、使用期間定位（Apple 規定讀取 Wi-Fi 名稱需要此權限；名稱僅用於告訴對方要連哪個網路，定位不會被使用或儲存）。
